## Who is this app for?

Reliability Engineer at an industrial facility. Starts the day reviewing daily shift reports and alarms. When an anomaly is flagged on a piece of equipment (e.g. a pump or compressor), investigates its history, current state and relevant documentation to brief the maintenance team. Works primarily at a desk in an office, occasionally visits the control room, on a standard desktop or laptop with a large monitor.

## What problem does this solve?

Once an asset such as PUMP-101 is flagged, the engineer has to log into SAP for open work orders, open a separate historian tool for live sensor data, and dig through a messy SharePoint drive for the P&ID. This takes 1-2 hours per asset, so they often rely on outdated Excel spreadsheets, ask colleagues on Teams for tribal knowledge, or decide with incomplete data to save time.

## Tasks and success

**One-sentence story.** As a Reliability Engineer, I want one view of a flagged asset's work orders, live sensor data and P&ID so that I can go from alarm to a complete maintenance briefing in minutes instead of 1-2 hours.

**Success criteria.** A Reliability Engineer completes an asset investigation (search the tag, view the live time-series chart, open a linked manual or P&ID from the same page) within 5 minutes instead of about 2 hours. Usage target: 20 queries per day per user. The old method of opening 3-4 siloed applications is abandoned.
